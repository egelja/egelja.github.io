With [Mingyoung Jessica Jeng](https://www.mingyoungjeng.com/) (equal contribution), Connor Selna, Michael Gavrincea, [Prof. Kaitlin N. Smith](https://knsmith.github.io/), and [Prof. Nikos Hardavellas](https://users.cs.northwestern.edu/~hardav/).

Proposes a compilation framework for modular quantum chiplet architectures — systems combining multiple smaller quantum processors to overcome scalability limits of monolithic devices. The compiler partitions and maps quantum circuits across chiplets, handling inter-chiplet communication.

Available on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.08478) (quant-ph).
