Accepted to the [IEEE International Conference on Quantum Computing and Engineering (QCE)](https://qce.quantum.ieee.org/) 2026 in Toronto.

Co-first-author (equal contribution) with <AuthorList names={["Mingyoung Jessica Jeng"]} />, alongside <AuthorList names={["Mu-Te Lau", "Connor Selna", "Michael Gavrincea", "Kaitlin N. Smith", "Nikos Hardavellas"]} />.

SEQC is a compilation framework for modular quantum chiplet architectures — systems combining multiple smaller quantum processors to overcome scalability limits of monolithic devices. The compiler partitions and maps quantum circuits across chiplets, handling inter-chiplet communication.

Available on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.08478) (first posted January 2025 as *Modular Compilation for Quantum Chiplet Architectures*).
