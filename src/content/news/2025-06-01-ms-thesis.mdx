My master's thesis investigated improved prefetching techniques for linked data structures — pointer-intensive structures where sequential prefetching fails due to irregular memory access patterns.

Advised by [Prof. Nikos Hardavellas](https://users.cs.northwestern.edu/~hardav/), with committee members [Prof. Peter Dinda](http://pdinda.org/) and [Prof. Russ Joseph](https://www.eecs.northwestern.edu/~rjoseph/).

Available on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.21669) (cs.AR) and the [Northwestern repository](https://doi.org/10.21985/n2-bsav-a158).
